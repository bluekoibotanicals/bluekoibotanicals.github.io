A closer look

Choose this butter when you want the texture to be the star, or when you prefer your body care to leave room for your own favorite fragrance. There are no added essential oils or perfume; you may still notice the natural scent of the butters and oils. Warm a small scoop between your hands and massage into dry areas. Arrowroot helps soften the oily feel of this rich, concentrated blend.

How to use

Massage a small amount onto clean, dry skin.

Ingredients
  • Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter
  • Limnanthes Alba (Meadowfoam) Seed Oil
  • Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil
  • Theobroma Cacao (Cocoa) Seed Butter
  • Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil
  • Maranta Arundinacea Root Powder
  • Cetyl Alcohol
  • Cera Alba (Beeswax)
  • Tocopherol (Vitamin E)
Storage & care

Store in a cool, dry place.

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
