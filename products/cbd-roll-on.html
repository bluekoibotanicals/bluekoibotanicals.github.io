A closer look

Glide the roller directly over clean, dry skin for a smooth, targeted application without dipping into a jar. MCT and jojoba oils give the formula its easy slip, while bisabolol and vitamin E help condition the skin. Menthol adds a cooling sensation, balanced by the fresh herbal scent of eucalyptus and lavender essential oils. Tuck the 10 mL bottle into your everyday bag or keep it beside your desk for a small moment of care wherever the day takes you.

How to use

Roll a small amount onto clean, dry skin and gently massage in if desired. Begin with a small area to check skin tolerance. Replace the cap after use.
