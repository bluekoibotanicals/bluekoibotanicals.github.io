A closer look

A small swipe is all it takes to make this little tube part of your daily routine. Spearmint brings the freshness, lavender adds an herbal softness, and meadowfoam and jojoba oils give the buttery base a comfortable glide. Slip it into your pocket for a minty refresh on the go, or pair it with Honey Lavender to have a balm for each mood.

How to use

Apply to lips as needed.

Ingredients
  • Cera Alba (Beeswax)
  • Theobroma Cacao (Cocoa) Seed Butter
  • Limnanthes Alba (Meadowfoam) Seed Oil
  • Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter
  • Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil
  • Tocopherol (Vitamin E)
  • Lavandula Angustifolia (Lavender) Oil
  • Mentha Spicata (Spearmint) Leaf Oil
Storage & care

Keep in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight.

For external use only. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
