A closer look

A few drops spread smoothly over damp skin, making it easy to give your arms and legs a little extra attention. The scent opens fresh and herbal, then feels more rounded as the warm, sweet notes come through. Finished with vitamin E, this is an inviting choice for mornings when you want your body care to feel as fresh as your clean skin.

How to use

Massage onto clean, damp skin.

Ingredients
  • Limnanthes Alba (Meadowfoam) Seed Oil
  • Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil
  • Tocopherol (Vitamin E)
  • Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil (Eucalyptus Essential Oil)
  • Lavandula Angustifolia (Lavender) Oil (Lavender Essential Oil)
  • Fragrance (Parfum) (Vanilla Fragrance Oil)
  • Juniperus Virginiana Oil (Cedarwood Essential Oil)
  • Fragrance (Parfum) (Honey Fragrance Oil)

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
