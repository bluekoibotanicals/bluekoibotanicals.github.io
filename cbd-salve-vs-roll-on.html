CBD salve or roll-on?

Choose Blue Koi’s CBD salve if you prefer a firm balm you can scoop and massage into skin. Choose the CBD roll-on if you prefer a liquid oil applied directly with a roller. Both are topical formats, made by hand in Virginia.

Read the amount and the size together.

The CBD amount on the label is the total for the entire container. A larger total does not, on its own, mean a more concentrated product or a different effect. Our salve sizes use weight in ounces and the roll-on uses volume in milliliters; those units should not be compared as if they were the same.

Check the ingredients before you choose.

Each product page includes the full ingredient list, directions and storage information. These products are for external use only. Follow the label, avoid broken or irritated skin, and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

A few useful details.

Is the roll-on an oral CBD oil?

No. The Blue Koi CBD Roll-On is a topical product for external use only and is not intended for human consumption. Follow the directions and warnings on the bottle.

Which ingredients are different?

The salve uses coconut oil, shea butter and beeswax for a firm balm. The roll-on uses MCT and jojoba oils with bisabolol and vitamin E for a liquid format. Both contain menthol and eucalyptus; the roll-on also lists lavender, and the salves list camphor. Read the complete ingredient list on the product page.

Where can I read the CBD lab report?

Our lab report page explains the CBD isolate report available on this site and links to the original PDF. Ask us about the report that matches your product and batch; an ingredient report is not a test of a finished product.
