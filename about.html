The person behind Blue Koi

It started
with my mom.

I learned to make body care through my mom’s business in Colorado. When I started Blue Koi Botanicals, I brought those skills with me.

Today, I make our products in small batches here in Virginia: oils, salves, mists, lip balms, and whipped butters. Some are lightly floral, some are woodsy, and there’s an unscented butter if you prefer to keep things simple.

If you’ve stopped by our market table, thank you. If we haven’t met yet, I’d love for you to try something and let me know what you think.
