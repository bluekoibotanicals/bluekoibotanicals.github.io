Good to know

Terms & cancellations.

Orders

Blue Koi Botanicals LLC makes products in small batches. Product descriptions, prices, and availability may change. We will confirm your order and contact you if an item is unavailable or if an order needs to be adjusted. Applicable taxes and shipping are communicated before payment.

Cancellations and changes

To request a cancellation or change, email blue.koi.botanicals@gmail.com as soon as possible with your name and order number. We can cancel an order before it ships and will refund the amount paid to the original payment method. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be canceled; please contact us if you need help with it. Refund processing times depend on the payment provider and your bank.

Defective products and returns

We accept returns of defective products. Email us your order number and a description of the issue; a photo is helpful. We will arrange a replacement and pay for return shipping and replacement. Please contact us before returning a product so we can provide instructions. Refunds are also available for defective items. Nothing in this policy limits rights available under applicable law.

Product use

Products are for external use only. Review the ingredient list before use and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Our products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

Shipping and privacy

See our shipping and returns policy and privacy notice for more information.
